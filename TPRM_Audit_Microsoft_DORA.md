# Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) Audit: CTPP Oversight
**Alignment:** DORA Article 28-30 (Management of ICT Third-Party Risk)
**Provider:** Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited (Designated CTPP)

## 1. Audit Scope
Verification of contractual alignment between the Financial Entity and Microsoft as a Critical Third-Party Provider.

## 2. Key Compliance Markers
- **Article 30 (Contractual Provisions):** Verified that the Microsoft Products and Services Data Protection Addendum (DPA) includes specific termination rights and audit access for EU regulators.
- **Concentration Risk:** Evaluated the reliance on Azure for all critical functions. 
- **Mitigation Strategy:** Implemented "Sovereign Cloud" redundancy using a multi-region Active-Active architecture to prevent single-point-of-failure at the provider level.

## 3. Evidence of Monitoring
- Quarterly review of **Microsoft Compliance Manager** scores.
- Monthly analysis of **Service Health Dashboards** for proactive disruption management.

## 4. Auditor Conclusion
Contractual and technical controls are in place to mitigate ICT third-party risk. Strategy remains compliant with EBA and ESMA guidelines for critical outsourcing.
