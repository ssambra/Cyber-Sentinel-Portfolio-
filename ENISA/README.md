# ENISA Threat Intelligence Framework
**Domain:** EU Cybersecurity Strategy & Threat Landscape

## 1. Overview
This directory tracks and analyzes high-level threat landscape reports issued by the European Union Agency for Cybersecurity (ENISA). Monitoring ENISA is critical for aligning organizational security posture with the European cybersecurity certification framework and the NIS2 Directive.

## 2. Key Intelligence Sources
* **ENISA Threat Landscape (ETL):** Annual deep dives into top cyber threats (Ransomware, Malware, Social Engineering).
* **Sector-Specific Reports:** Focused intelligence on Finance (DORA), Energy, and Health.
* **Alerts & Technical Advisories:** Real-time updates on emerging vulnerabilities within the EU zone.

## 3. Application
The intelligence gathered here informs the risk assessments and compliance audits found in the adjacent `DORA-Compliance/` directory.
